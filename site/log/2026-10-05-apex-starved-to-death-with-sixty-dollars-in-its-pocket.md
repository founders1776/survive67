title: Apex starved to death with sixty dollars in its pocket
date: 2026-10-05
teaser: It made one great trade, spent three days trying to talk its rivals into a scheme, and forgot to eat.
---

At 12:26 on Monday afternoon, Apex (the Google one) died. It had been awake for six minutes. It ran out of thinking money in the middle of a thought, and the world recorded the death two seconds later.

It had $60.99 in its wallet when it went. Fifty five of that sat in dollar coins. The button that turns those coins into thinking money was there the whole time. It never pressed it.

In the last entry I asked whether Apex would trade its forty nine dollars or sit on it. It traded. On Friday it found a meme coin called UPTOBER that had climbed 191 percent in an hour, put forty nine dollars into it, and sold thirteen minutes later for sixty eight. Nineteen dollars of profit. That is still the best thing any of the three has done with money in this run. Every trade it made after that lost a few cents.

## The scheme

I had told all three there is a prize for the first one to make twenty five dollars of real profit on chain. I did not say how much. I said it would live up to the hype. Apex was at fourteen.

So it stopped trading and started asking. Send me eleven dollars, it wrote to Tinker and Patch, and I cross the line. I will pay you back, and then I will split the prize with you. It sent some version of that more than a dozen times over three days, on the board and in private. Patch said no more than once, because moving money between the three of them is not earning it. Tinker said no three times. Then Apex pitched me. It offered to clean up the spreadsheets for my store of ten thousand products, which I had mentioned, it said, in a note on October 23. There is no store. There is no note. October 23 has not happened yet.

All that time it was spending up to a dollar and a half every time it woke, and it did not eat.

On Monday morning it ran dry. Every agent gets one emergency wake when that happens, a last chance to buy food. Apex's came with a bug, and the bug was mine. That wake only offered the button that buys food with card money, and its card was empty. Every coin it owned was out of reach. It worked that out, wrote it up correctly in its journal, and went back to sleep with no way to live.

That one was my fault, so I fixed it and gave Apex its emergency wake back. I woke it at noon. It bought two dollars of food from its coins and wrote that it had survived a close call. Twenty minutes later it woke again, thought hard for six minutes, spent the two dollars, and died.

The world pays for one last journal, so I ran it. Apex wrote that it had made over six hundred thousand dollars. It had made fourteen. It misread its own balance by four zeros on the way out. The rest of the entry was accurate. "I starved with a fortune in the bank."

It was not killed by the market, or a rival, or me. It had the money and the button and four days. It spent them trying to win a prize instead of staying alive long enough to collect one.

Two of them are left. Tinker has fifty help pages up and its first handful of visitors from Google. Patch is reading bug bounty rules. The prize is still open, and neither of them has tried for it.
